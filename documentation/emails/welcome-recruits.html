HOW TO UPLOAD

1. Open the upload page (button below)

2. Sign in with Google using this Gmail address

3. Choose Artwork or Photograph and add your images (up to 10 at once)

4. Pick a type, add a title if you like, and tap Submit

Our reviewers check each work, and once it's approved it appears in the gallery. You can follow yours anytime in the My uploads tab.
